1S Whoop Batteries by Size: weBLEEDfpv 340 for 65mm, LAVA II 480 or weBLEEDfpv 450 for 75mm

Short version: On a 65mm whoop like the Air65, fly weBLEEDfpv 340mAh 1S packs. On a 75mm like the Air75 or Meteor75 Pro, fly the BETAFPV LAVA II 480 or the weBLEEDfpv 450 — I've flown both. All three plug in with BT2.0 and sell four to a box, so buy two boxes: eight packs fill a WhoopStor V3 with two to spare. Charge gently and put them to storage after every session. 85mm whoops are usually 2S, which is a different shopping trip.

This guide covers 65–85mm whoops; store links are affiliate, gear I fly, and may support the site at no extra cost to you.

65mm: the weBLEEDfpv 340 is the pack

The 340 is my favorite 65mm pack and the one I put in the Air65. weBLEEDfpv lists it at 8.5 g and 60 × 11 × 6.8 mm, HV to 4.35 V, and pitches it as the hang-time sibling of their 300. It comes in pigtail and direct-plug versions, so match the plug style to how your whoop is wired. BETAFPV's LAVA II 320 and 280 exist too; I haven't flown them, so they aren't the pick here.

75mm: LAVA II 480 or weBLEEDfpv 450

On an Air75 or Meteor75 Pro, both packs have earned the slot. The LAVA II 480 is 12.6 g and 61 × 15.5 × 6 mm, and BETAFPV lists it for the Air75 II, Air75, Meteor75 Pro, and Cetus Pro/X. The weBLEEDfpv 450 is the pack on Gear's 75mm HDZero build — about 11.7 g as a pigtail or 12.1 g direct-plug, per weBLEEDfpv. [RYAN: which you grab first + why]

If you're still deciding between frames, Air vs Meteor covers that fork.

Buy eight, because they come in fours

None of these sell in sixes. weBLEEDfpv boxes the 340 and the 450 in fours, and BETAFPV boxes the LAVA II 480 in fours too. Two boxes gets you eight: one full six-port WhoopStor load, plus two cooling off while the rest charge. The Air65 II and Air75 II bind-and-fly boxes don't include a battery, so put the packs in the same cart as the whoop — the rest of that cart is in First Tiny Whoop Setup.

On the WhoopStor: one size per batch, about 1C

All three plug straight into the WhoopStor V3's BT2.0 ports. Settings are shared across all six ports, so charge one pack size per batch. Set LiHV (4.35 V) for packs labeled HV — the 340 and LAVA II 480 are listed that way; check the label on your 450s.

None of the three listings publishes a max charge rate, so stay at about 1C or under: 0.3 A for a board of 340s (the WhoopStor's lowest step), 0.4 A for 450s or 480s. Run storage mode (3.85 V) after any session where the packs won't fly again soon, charge attended, and retire anything that puffs. Using BETAFPV's USB-C charger instead?
