WhoopStor V3 vs BETAFPV HexaCharger — Keep the Default or Go USB-C Kit?

Short version: For a first 1S whoop fleet, QuadMath still defaults to WhoopStor V3. HexaCharger (or HexaCharger Pro) is a fine USB-C desk fork when you already live on capable PD and do not need XT60 or field power — not a reason to overturn the default. Need per-port control or the flexible/power path? That comparison lives on WhoopStor vs W63; this page only answers the Hexa cart question.

This guide covers 65–85mm whoops. Chargers are name-only until Gear has /go/ cards. Specs below are manufacturer / Oscar Liang claims — not QuadMath amp benches.

What both do well

Six ports, PH2.0 and BT2.0 (one connector per channel), LiPo 4.20 V / LiHV 4.35 V, storage mode, and a color screen. Both beat a dumb USB brick. Both also share the same trap: current and target voltage are shared across ports — independent channels, not independent settings.

The real fork: power input and current steps

WhoopStor V3 HexaCharger / Pro
Power inUSB-C PD, XT60, DC 5.5×2.1USB-C only
Charge current0.3–1.3 A, 0.1 A steps0.5 A min, 0.5 A steps (Oscar); Pro up to 3 A all ports
Storage3.80 / 3.85 V3.85 V
PD class~40 W for full capability; not plain 5 V USBNon-Pro ≥30 W; Pro ≥100 W (BETAFPV)

WhoopStor still wins when you might charge from a field LiPo or a barrel brick. Hexa wins when the desk already has a real PD supply and you will never miss XT60. On ~300 mAh packs, WhoopStor can dial a true ~0.3 A (about 1C); Hexa’s 0.5 A floor is the gentlest step Oscar documents — workable, just not as fine.

Keep WhoopStor V3 if…

You are buying the first multi-pack charger for a matched 65mm fleet, want XT60/DC later, prefer 0.1 A steps for gentle currents, or simply want to stay on QuadMath’s beginner path. That is the locked default here.

HexaCharger is fine if…

Your cart already pairs a Hexa with a USB-C whoop desk life, you own a capable PD brick, you do not need field XT60, and shared settings plus 0.5 A steps are acceptable. Storage at 3.85 V is real — this is still a smart multi-port charger.

Pro vs non-Pro: Pro’s ≥100 W PD and up-to-3 A-all-ports headroom is overkill for many 65mm ~300 mAh packs. Non-Pro with ≥30 W PD is enough for gentle whoop currents for most buyers. Do not upsell Pro by default.

Caveats worth one pass

Buy a real PD adapter (Hexa packages usually do not include one). Plain phone 5 V USB will not unlock either charger’s full capability. Match LiPo vs LiHV to the pack label; do not slam 3 A into small packs. Soft tip from Oscar’s Hexa review: reverse-insert can fry a port, and he observed no reverse-polarity protection — check PH2.0/BT2.0 orientation and do not force plugs. Charge attended on a non-flammable surface.
