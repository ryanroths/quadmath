        How the numbers are made
        
    
          
  • Flight gating. Only samples above 15% throttle feed the spectra and step response. Bench idling is not flying.
    • 
          
  • Noise spectra. Welch PSD: Hann window, 50% overlap, 1024-point FFT at 2 kHz+ (shorter at lower log rates). Peaks are reported by prominence over a running 80 Hz median floor, so a broad hump does not count and a narrow resonance does.
    • 
          
  • Step response. Wiener deconvolution of setpoint → gyro over 2 s windows with 10% stride, Tukey-windowed and zero-padded, regularised above 25 Hz. Only windows with real stick input are kept, weighted by amplitude. Same family of method as PID-Analyzer and PIDtoolbox.
    • 
          
  • Propwash. Pitch tracking error in 20–90 Hz after a throttle chop (fell > 35% in 0.5 s) versus the same band in mid-throttle cruise.
    • 
          
  • Motor saturation. Share of in-flight samples with any motor at ≥ 98% of its output range.
    • 
          
  • Parser. blackbox-log (MIT) compiled to WebAssembly. Betaflight and INAV logs; EmuFlight is untested.
