Methodology

Whoop-scale current sensors are not trustworthy out of the box — one of these two builds read 20% cold, the other 25% hot. Every current and mAh figure on this page is corrected before it is published.

WeightsKitchen scale, measured per component. Pack weight averaged over two packs.
DurationStopwatch, plug-in to landing at load cutoff. No sensor involved — no correction needed.
Discharge window4.35 V LiHV full charge down to ~3.3 V under load, assumed 270 mAh delivered of a nominal 300.
Current correctionFull-discharge anchor: raw OSD mAh scaled so the run totals the assumed 270 mAh delivered. Per-build correction factor stored in the raw JSON.
UncertaintyRoughly ±8% on current and mAh figures, driven by the assumed delivered capacity. Durations and weights carry no such caveat.
Flight styleCruise — slow, steady laps. Hover and freestyle numbers pending; freestyle will run meaningfully shorter.

When your inputs in the calculator match one of these builds, it swaps its estimate for the measured number. Everything on this page is reproducible from the raw JSON in data/bench/. More builds get added as they cross the bench.
