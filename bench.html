Bench Data

The calculator is a model. This page is not. Every number here was measured — builds weighed on a scale, timed with a stopwatch, current read off the onboard sensor. Three builds so far across two frame classes. The two 65mm builds were flown to a full discharge against a known-capacity anchor and their corrected figures are what QuadMath's 65mm estimates rest on. The 75mm build is provisional: its current sensor has never been calibrated and neither run was controlled, so its current and mAh figures are published raw and feed nothing.

Methodology

Whoop-scale current sensors are not trustworthy out of the box — one 65mm build read 20% cold, the other 25% hot. The policy is that every current and mAh figure gets corrected against a full-discharge anchor before it is published.

One build on this page does not meet that bar yet. The 75mm TreeTopper has never been flown to a full discharge, so it has no anchor, no correction factor, and no uncertainty figure. The choice there is publish it raw or withhold it, and withholding it is the worse of the two — a missing build teaches nothing, while a flagged one still carries usable durations and weights. So it is published raw: every uncorrected figure is marked Provisional on the row, rendered in amber with a "raw, uncorrected" marker on the cell, and spelled out in full under the build's own record. Provisional builds are excluded from cross-build comparisons and never feed a calculator estimate. They lose the flag when the sensor is calibrated and the runs are re-flown controlled — not before.

WeightsKitchen scale, measured per component. Pack weight averaged over two packs.
DurationStopwatch, plug-in to landing at load cutoff. No sensor involved — no correction needed.
Discharge window4.35 V LiHV full charge down to ~3.3 V under load, assumed 270 mAh delivered of a nominal 300.
Current correctionFull-discharge anchor: raw OSD mAh scaled so the run totals the assumed 270 mAh delivered. Per-build correction factor stored in the raw JSON. A null factor means no anchor run exists — that build is provisional and its figures are raw.
Controlled runsSame charge state, same cutoff, repeatable throttle profile. A run failing any of those is marked "controlled": false in the raw JSON and cannot be compared against another run.
UncertaintyRoughly ±8% on corrected current and mAh figures, driven by the assumed delivered capacity. Raw figures on provisional builds carry no stated uncertainty — the sensor error is unmeasured, and the two calibrated builds bracket it at 20% cold to 25% hot. Durations and weights carry no such caveat on any build.
Flight styleCruise — slow, steady laps — is the only style with anchor-grade data behind it. Hover pending. Freestyle numbers now exist on the 75mm build, but they are provisional and anchor nothing. A freestyle run is shorter than a cruise run on the same build, so freestyle and cruise durations are marked on the cell and never compared on this page.

When your inputs in the calculator match one of the calibrated builds, it swaps its estimate for the measured number. Provisional builds are not wired in — the 75mm TreeTopper changes no estimate. Everything on this page is reproducible from the raw JSON in data/bench/. More builds get added as they cross the bench.
