Shipped files are a small meshopt-compressed geometry subset (no colormap
textures — the sim paints Volt Stall materials for bloom). Total GLB
payload is 51 128 bytes (49.9 KB). Low quality fetches only the bench,
chest, and crate (17 384 bytes). Med/High fetch the rest.

  workbench.glb   Survival Kit workbench
  chest.glb       Survival Kit chest
  crate.glb       Survival Kit box
  toolbox.glb     Furniture Kit kitchenCabinetDrawer
  cardboard.glb   Furniture Kit cardboardBoxClosed
  tire.glb        Car Kit wheel-dark
  sedan.glb       Car Kit sedan
  barrel.glb      Survival Kit barrel
  barrier.glb     Racing Kit barrierRed

Nothing here is from the GPL whoop sim. Procedural Volt Stall stays the
fallback if a GLB fails to load.
