Support Kenney: https://kenney.nl/donate  (credit is not required)

Shipped files are a small meshopt-compressed geometry subset (no colormap
textures — the sim paints neon emissive materials for bloom). Total GLB
payload is 80 532 bytes (78.6 KB). Low quality fetches only the four cars
(54 304 bytes). Med/High fetch the rest.

  sedan.glb           Car Kit sedan
  hatch.glb           Car Kit hatchback-sports
  van.glb             Car Kit van

  building-wide.glb   City Kit Commercial low-detail-building-wide-a
  awning.glb          City Kit Commercial detail-awning-wide

  stop-sign.glb       City Kit Roads road-sign-stop
  street-lamp.glb     City Kit Roads light-curved

Nothing here is from WebFPV. Procedural Neon Current stays the fallback
if a GLB fails to load.
