Shipped files are a small meshopt-compressed geometry subset (no colormap
textures — the sim paints Ion Nave materials for bloom). Total payload is
11 616 bytes (11.3 KB). Low quality fetches only the crate (3 708 bytes).
Med/High fetch barrel and cardboard too.

  crate.glb       Survival Kit box
  barrel.glb      Survival Kit barrel
  cardboard.glb   Furniture Kit cardboardBoxClosed

Nothing here is from the GPL whoop sim. Procedural Ion Nave stays the
fallback if a mesh fails to load.
