Optimization
  GLBs: meshopt + mesh quantization via glTF-Transform 4.x
  Textures: 1k WebP (High/Med), 512 WebP albedo on Low
  LODs: Low fetches fence + pot only (Kenney trees/palms stay catalog-only;
        hero veg is procedural frond palms + leaf-card ficus so they do not
        read as lollipops)
  Instancing: fence posts, pots, bushes, sofa, AC
  Lazy-load: sim.html queues tryDressBackyardKit after first paint

Payload (folder, not counting shared looks textures):
  High / Med  ~2.5 MB  (HDRI 1.6 + tex 0.77 + GLB 0.08)
  Low         ~1.7 MB  (HDRI 1.6 + core GLB 0.02 + 512 albedo)
