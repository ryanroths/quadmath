Shipped files are a small meshopt-compressed geometry subset. Total
payload is 10 960 bytes. Low quality fetches only cardboard (2 792 bytes).

  cardboard.glb   Furniture Kit cardboardBoxClosed
  crate.glb       Survival Kit box
  toolbox.glb     Furniture Kit kitchenCabinetDrawer

Nothing here is from the GPL whoop sim. Procedural Clay Parlor stays the
fallback if a mesh fails to load.
